About Me

Bridging sports media, photography, and college life.

Hi, I'm Kaileigh!

I am a senior at Miami University in Oxford, Ohio, studying Sports Media and Communications. Alongside my studies, I run Field & Frame Co., a small business specializing in sports media, brand design, and visual storytelling.

Whether capturing high-stakes athletic moments or crafting custom brand graphics, I bring energy, creativity, and precision to every project.

Highlights & Experience

  • Worked as an intern for the Dayton Dragons.
  • Manages and curates content for the Monroe High School Volleyball Instagram.
  • Specializes in custom athletic graphics, game day posts, and event flyers.
  • Offers professional photography packages for sports action, senior portraits, and couples.
